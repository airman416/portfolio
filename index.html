SamGPT + Viral Clip Generator

Visit ↗

Search every Sam Parr quote on My First Million in plain English, then turn the moment you find into a ready-to-post clip.

For
Sam Parr, co-host of My First Million, and the editors who cut his clips.
Problem
The best moments are buried past minute forty of three-hour episodes. Finding them meant scrubbing by hand.
What it does
Ask a question and get the exact quote with a YouTube link to the second it was said. Paste any episode link and get its 3 best moments, each under 2 minutes, cut to 16:9.

02

Hookline

hookline.app ↗

Get app installs from TikTok without making TikToks. Hookline writes, designs and posts slideshows, then learns which ones brought installs.

For
Solo app founders who need users but have no time or skill for daily content.
Problem
Slideshows are the cheapest way to grow an app on TikTok, but making 7 good slides every day is a full-time job, and AI-made posts usually look fake.
What it does
Ten minutes of setup. Each day you pick from drafts that already pass its copy rules. It posts at the best time and uses install numbers to write the next batch.
Status
Live, paid product ($49/month).
Built with
Claude APINode + ExpressCanvas renderingReactSupabaseTikTok API
03

makeapps.world

makeapps.world ↗

Turn raw phone screenshots into App Store screenshots that sell, in minutes, in the browser.

For
Indie iOS developers without a designer.
Problem
Shoppers decide on an app from its first screenshot. A designer costs $300+ and a week; raw screenshots lose installs.
What it does
Upload screens and get a full set with device frames, headlines and backgrounds, exported at Apple's sizes. AI writes the headlines; the layout is fixed in code so every slide fits.
Status
Live. Free to start, no account needed.
Built with
Next.jsSupabaseClaude APIHeadless Chromium export

swipefile

Visit ↗

A library of the web's best landing pages, 652 sections from 211 sites, sorted by part of the page.

For
Designers and founders building a landing page.
Problem
To design a pricing section, you want to see how 30 great sites did theirs. Collecting them by hand takes hours.
What it does
A scraper crawls from site to similar site, saves each section in full resolution and tags it (hero, pricing, call to action). The library grows on its own.
